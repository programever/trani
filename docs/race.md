# The race: Dinh Harvest Final 2026

Looked up by Alpha on 2026-10-22 on the organizer's registration page and in
the rules (Trail Station). Iker asked to keep this in the repo, not in Alpha's
memory. Iker is registered for the 65 km.

- Sunday 27 December 2026 at Núi Dinh. Start and finish at Hồ Bên Suối (the
  Expo). Five distances: 10, 20, 30, 45 and 65 km.
- **65 km: start 02:00, finish by 18:00. The time limit is 16 hours.** Meeting
  and bag drop 01:00-01:45, warm-up and safety talk 01:45.
- 16 hours for 65 km is 14 min 45 s per km on average, stops included.
  Target: 13 min per km, about 14 hours, two hours to spare. Stops at aid
  stations: 5 minutes at most.
- **Climb: not published yet.** The registration page shows "about 3500 m" as
  a first number; the organizer says the numbers may change before race day.
  The 45 km shows about 2360 m.
- About four hours in the dark at the start; the sun rises around 06:00. A
  bright headlamp is mandatory for 45 and 65 km. The last hours are in the
  midday heat.
- Mandatory gear for 65 km (missing: 30 minutes added, or disqualified):
  water bottle or bladder of 1.5 litres, vest or pack, whistle, phone,
  first-aid kit (elastic bandage, sterile gauze, plasters, painkiller,
  emergency blanket), headlamp.
- Aid stations give porridge and soft drinks; runners bring their own bowl,
  spoon and cup. Drop bag at the Expo, back at the finish. Buses back to the
  city 12:00-18:30.
- The organizer may stop a runner at a checkpoint when the runner cannot
  finish in time. No checkpoint cut-offs are published, only the 16 hours.
- Entry requirement for 65 km: a 25 km trail finish in the last year.
- Other distances: 45 km starts 05:00 (13 hours), 30 km 06:00 (10 hours),
  20 km 06:30 (7 hours), 10 km 07:00 (5 hours).
- Page: https://tikpik.vn/events/dinh-harvest-final-2026/ (the rules PDF is
  linked there).
