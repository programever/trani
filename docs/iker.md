## What this means for the plan

- The good day (July) was 10.5 min/km with 1900 m of climb. The slow days were
  14 and 15.3 min/km. The 65 km race allows 14 min 45 s per km on average.
  So the plan's first job is fewer cramps, not more speed: a slow first half,
  salt and food every hour, strong calves and front thighs, long days on the
  mountain by day, and the last hours of those days in the heat.
- The daily plan (see the README) makes the next training day lighter when
  Iker marks a day "Chuột rút".
