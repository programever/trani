new Claude session still knows what happened before.

plan. When Iker says something about a training day (a result, a cramp, pain,

the same day, then commit and push. The newest day is at the top. Write facts:
what was planned, what Iker did, the numbers Iker sent, what went wrong, and
what was decided.

not about training.

are listed here only when Alpha copies them in.
